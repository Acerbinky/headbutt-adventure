How to play

← → or A D — run. Space / W — jump (hold for higher).

Z or J — headbutt! Smash enemies, crack blocks, bonk fruit loose.

In the air: tap Z again and again to spin your head and float down. ↓ + Z — diving headbutt.

Grab a vine and press ↑ ↓ to climb with your teeth, caveman style.

Meat on the bone powers you up: Bonk → Big Bonk → MEGA BONK. Bigger bonks crack dark rock. Candy shrinks you tiny — handy for treasure tunnels.

Stomp enemies from above, but never stomp the spiky one. Reach the stone arch to clear each level… and something huge stomps around Dino Peak.

A love letter to the big-headed caveman platformers of the 16-bit era. · Tip the developer
